NextGen2 has a built-in assistant you can ask questions in plain English.

## What it can answer

- Questions about your data: how many tests are failing, what coverage looks
  like, which suite last ran.
- Questions about using NextGen2, answered from the help articles and any
  training documents that have been uploaded.

When it answers a how-to question it cites the article and version it used.
The article is the authority; the answer is a convenience.

## What it will not do

- It will not cite a draft article. Unapproved content is not authority.
- It will not invent an answer. If nothing in the help content matches your
  question, it says so and offers to raise a ticket. Your question is recorded
  so the missing article can be written.
- It will not carry out an action without showing you what it is about to do
  and waiting for confirmation.
